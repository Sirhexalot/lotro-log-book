The Lord of the Rings Online

A map that shows where quests have their objectives.

Region by region, next to your own progress. No account, no server, no invented markers.

How it works

  1. Pick a region. The tree on the left: region, area, sub-area. Each row shows how many quests exist there and how many you have finished.
  2. Click a quest. The text with every step and objective appears on the right. On the map, objectives are dots: gold = open, green = done.
  3. Look where to go. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan. With a quest selected, numbered rings mark its individual objectives.

Create your progress file

For the numbers you need your Progress.lua. The tool reads it from the running game; it changes nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Download the tool (12 KB)

  1. Unpack. Extract the ZIP into its own folder.
  2. Start the game. Magic Quest Log must be installed and the game running.
  3. Run the starter. Windows: read-journal.bat, macOS: read-journal.command.
  4. Choose the file. Load the resulting Progress.lua on the map with “Load progress”.

Your progress

The numbers in the tree and the colour of the dots come from Progress.lua for your character. The file lives in the plugin folder under MagicQuestLog/Data/ and is read when the page is built (build_daten.py).

Without it the map still works — it simply shows no numbers and leaves the dots uncoloured. No zero is invented.

What the map cannot do

  • It shows objective locations, not quest givers. Where you accept a quest is not part of the game data.
  • Of 15,209 quests, 8,403 have a verified position. The rest are listed in the tree and the quest list but get no dot — better no marker than a wrong one.
  • It is not live. It knows your progress as of the export.
