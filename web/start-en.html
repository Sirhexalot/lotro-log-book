LOTRO Log Book

The Lord of the Rings Online

Your logbook for Middle-earth: where you still have to go – and what you have already done.

Quests and deeds on the game’s own maps, plus reputation and crafting – for every character you load from LOTRO Companion. No account, no upload, no invented markers.

What is in it

  1. Quests. 15,209 quests by region, with level range, quest giver, requirements, quest chain and LOTRO points. The map shows where a quest is given. Steps and objectives stay hidden until you choose Show objectives for a quest – so nothing gives the solution away too early. The available filter shows what your character can pick up right now. Below the map, Filter/Notes shows vendors, stable-masters, mailboxes and more, just like in the game.
  2. Deeds. 5,534 deeds with your running count, such as “44/75” – so you see how far along you are.
  3. Reputation. Your standing with every faction. One click shows which open quests and deeds still give reputation there.
  4. Crafting. Every profession tier by tier, proficiency and mastery, and what comes next.

Your progress

Progress, counters, reputation and crafting come from LOTRO Companion. On the Progress page you choose Companion’s folder – it is read only in your browser, nothing is uploaded. Several characters? Switch between them in the menu. The same page writes the files for the Magic Quest Log plugin.

To your progress

Without progress everything still works – just without numbers and colours. No zero is invented.

Collecting deeds above all? Have a look at the mature plugin Deed Tracker by b414213562.

What the logbook cannot do

  • For 6,621 of 15,209 quests we know where the quest giver stands, for 12,010 at least one objective. The rest are in the list, but get no dot – better no marker than a wrong one.
  • Indoor places have their own coordinates. Whatever stands inside does not appear on the map.
  • “Available” checks level and earlier quests. It cannot check class, race or reputation, and the game no longer offers some old quests.
  • It is not live. It knows your progress from the last Companion import.
