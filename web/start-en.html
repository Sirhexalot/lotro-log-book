LOTRO Log Book

The Lord of the Rings Online

Quests, deeds and your progress in Middle-earth.

Choose a landscape or open a book of the epic story. LOTRO Log Book brings together its quests and maps, plus deeds, reputation and crafting. No registration. You can load your progress from LOTRO Companion; the files stay in your browser.

Open landscapes and stories

Finding your way

  • Landscapes: alphabetical within each region. The links above jump to Eriador, Rhovanion, Gondor and the other regions. Open sub-areas from their card or above the map.
  • Stories and other content: browse the epic story by volume or series, then book. Festivals, crafting, instances and missions have their own groups. Other quests are collected at the bottom.
  • List, map, details: search quests and areas. Selecting a quest leaves your chosen map in place. Drag the gold divider to widen the list; double-click to reset it.

What is in it

  1. Quests. 15,118 catalogue entries with level ranges, quest givers, known requirements, quest chains and LOTRO points where the data provides them. Steps and objectives stay hidden until you choose Show objectives for a quest. The available filter checks your imported progress and known requirements. Below the map, Filter/Notes shows vendors, stable-masters, mailboxes and more.
  2. Deeds. 5,528 deeds in the catalogue. Imported progress adds matching objective counters, such as “44/75”.
  3. Reputation. Your standing with every faction. One click shows which open quests and deeds still give reputation there.
  4. Crafting. Every profession tier by tier, proficiency and mastery, and what comes next.

Your progress

Progress, counters, reputation and crafting come from LOTRO Companion. On the Progress page you choose Companion’s folder – it is read only in your browser, nothing is uploaded. Several characters? Switch between them in the menu. The same page writes the files for the Magic Quest Log plugin.

To your progress

You can browse without importing anything. Catalogue totals are still visible; your personal progress remains unknown.

Collecting deeds above all? Have a look at the mature plugin Deed Tracker by b414213562.

What the logbook cannot do

  • 6,860 quests have a recorded quest-giver location; 12,444 have at least one objective location. Missing locations get no marker. Some groups still have no map assigned; their quests remain accessible in the list.
  • Catalogue totals are not a count of currently playable quests. Repeatable content, alternative entry points and old content may be included.
  • Indoor places have their own coordinates. Whatever stands inside does not appear on the map.
  • “Available” checks level and earlier quests. It cannot check class, race or reputation, and the game no longer offers some old quests.
  • It is not live. It knows your progress from the last Companion import.
